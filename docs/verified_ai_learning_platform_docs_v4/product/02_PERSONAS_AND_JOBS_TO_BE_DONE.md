# Personas and Jobs To Be Done

## Why personas matter architecturally

Personas determine onboarding fields, default explanation depth, exam workflows, pricing sensitivity, tutor tone, analytics segmentation and accessibility. We do not build for an undefined "student."

## Persona A — Independent High-School Learner

### Context
Uses school notes, YouTube, ChatGPT, calculator tools and homework apps.

### Jobs
- Check whether a solution is correct.
- Understand the exact wrong step.
- Prepare for an exam.
- Spend time on weak topics rather than already-mastered material.

### Pain
- Generic AI forgets the broader learning journey.
- Explanations may not match the student's level.
- Past mistakes disappear into chat history.
- Student does not know what to revise next.

### Product response
- verified status,
- concise step explanations,
- automatic mistake book,
- daily plan,
- syllabus mapping.

## Persona B — University STEM Student

### Context
Works with calculus, linear algebra, probability, statistics and engineering mathematics.

### Jobs
- Verify symbolic results.
- Compare own derivation with a reference.
- Find the first divergence in a long solution.
- Prepare for midterms/finals.

### Pain
- A subtle AI hallucination can invalidate an entire derivation.
- Exact notation and domain constraints matter.
- Course methods differ from generic explanations.

### Product response
- symbolic verification,
- deep explanation mode,
- attempt-step comparison,
- course workspace in later phases,
- advanced verifier modules.

## Persona C — Exam-Driven Learner

### Jobs
- Know current readiness.
- Allocate limited daily time.
- Practice high-impact weak skills.
- Simulate exam conditions.
- Review mistakes shortly before the exam.

### Product response
- exam profile,
- weighted skill map,
- readiness estimate,
- adaptive study plan,
- mock exams,
- final review mode.

## Persona D — Parent-Supported Learner

Parent may fund the subscription but student owns the learning experience.

Potential later product behavior:
- high-level progress summary,
- privacy-preserving parent view,
- no default full transcript surveillance.

## Anti-personas

### Cheating-only user
The product is not optimized to bypass live assessment controls.

### Bulk API abuser
Automated high-volume use is constrained by quotas/rate limits.

### Generic assistant power user with no need for structure
May be better served by ChatGPT/Gemini. We do not distort the product to capture every AI use case.

## Core Jobs To Be Done

1. When I am stuck, help me understand the correct solution and show whether it was actually checked.
2. When I am wrong, tell me precisely why I am wrong instead of only revealing the right answer.
3. When I repeat similar mistakes, recognize the pattern automatically.
4. When I have limited time, decide what I should practice next.
5. When an exam approaches, identify risk areas and build a realistic plan.
6. When AI is uncertain, tell me instead of pretending certainty.

## Purchase motivations

Users are likely to pay for:
- verified solving at meaningful volume,
- deeper mistake analysis,
- full history,
- adaptive planning,
- exam mode,
- advanced reports,
- premium model fallback,
- PDF/course features later.

The product should demonstrate value before aggressively asking for payment.
