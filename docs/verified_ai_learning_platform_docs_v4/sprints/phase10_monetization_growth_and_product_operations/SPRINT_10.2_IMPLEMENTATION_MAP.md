|---|---|---|---|
| `ATTEMPT_VERIFY` | `FREE` | `AttemptEvaluationService.evaluate`, `AttemptWorkService.submit` | Allowed on `FREE`; `503 ENTITLEMENT_SYNC_FAILED` on billing outage |
| `DETERMINISTIC_TWIN` | `FREE` | `AttemptLearningService.create`, `CounterfactualTwinChallengeService` (deterministic path) | Allowed on `FREE`; existing drill replays preserved |
